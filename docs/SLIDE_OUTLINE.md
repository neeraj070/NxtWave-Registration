# Five-slide submission outline

## 1. The growth challenge

- Goal: 500 final-year engineering student registrations in seven days.
- Free workshop: “Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes”.
- ₹2,000 budget; student-led distribution plus a small paid test.
- **Visual:** Workshop landing page screenshot.
- **Speaker notes:** This app simulates the workflow; it does not claim the goal was achieved.

## 2. Channel strategy and assumptions

- 240 ambassadors, 120 clubs, 90 student communities, 20 paid, 30 recovery.
- Planned allocation totals 500; reach and conversion assumptions are unvalidated.
- **Visual:** Campaign Plan channel table.
- **Speaker notes:** Explain how the 7-day sequence moves from setup to review.

## 3. The measurable funnel

- Public registration form validates final-year eligibility and unique email.
- Referral codes and UTM sources resolve to one stored channel per registration.
- SQLite powers dashboard metrics and CSV exports.
- **Visual:** Registration page and channel chart screenshots.
- **Speaker notes:** Walk through one generated ambassador link and an example registration.

## 4. Dashboard measures and decision rules

- Daily pace, cost per registration, ambassador leaderboard, and budget remaining.
- Transparent recommendations include evidence status and guard against tiny samples.
- Demo records are synthetic, visibly labeled, and resettable.
- **Visual:** Overview dashboard and DEMO DATA banner screenshots.
- **Speaker notes:** Demonstrate using labeled synthetic data. Do not describe dashboard values as results from a real campaign.

## 5. What I would validate next

- Confirm event details and privacy/consent workflow before outreach.
- Test channel reach and conversion assumptions with small measured pilots.
- Improve session storage, CSRF defenses, monitoring, and deployment controls before public launch.
- **Visual:** Seven-day timeline or recommendations panel.
- **Speaker notes:** AI-assisted notes are draft reflections to verify against actual development work.
